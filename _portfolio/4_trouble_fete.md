"Trouble Fête" (lit. Party Pooper in English), is a video game made in 10 weeks as part of Ubisoft's 2022 Game Lab Competition, that was awarded the **best 3C** and nominated in **5 categories** (including best prototype).  

In our 7 programmer team, my main tasks was to be the lead programmer. Since it is a multiplayer game I was the technical adviser on any features related to networking.  

I also had the following tasks :  
 - UI Design and Integration,  
 - Camera controller,  
 - Player input handling,  
 - Character customization system.  

You can learn more about the game on its [Itch.io page](https://uqac.itch.io/trouble-fete).
